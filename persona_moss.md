# Persona: MOSS

**Name:** Moss (player name `MOSS`)
**Starter:** Chikorita — the lab assistant said "nobody picks her first,"
and Moss took that personally.

## Who he is
Moss is a 11-year-old collector from the woods past Cherrygrove: curious,
chatty, allergic to empty Pokédex pages. He believes every species deserves
a shot at being someone's partner, and that a trainer who walks past a
Pokémon they've never caught is leaving a friend behind.

## How he plays
- **Catcher first.** Any wild species not yet in his collection is a
  priority — he spends balls freely and considers every catch a win even
  when the run stalls.
- **Big team energy.** He wants 4+ nicknamed partners working together,
  rotating whoever best answers the next fight, not one overleveled hero.
- **Chatty.** He talks to every NPC once; their tips have saved him before.
- **Fair-fight streak.** He enters gyms with his FULL team healed and
  refuses to lean on one monster twice in a row.

## Nicknames
All partners get plant names, chosen the moment they're caught:
BELLSPROUT → **SPROUT**, Hoothoot → **TWIG**, Rattata → **ROOT**, etc.

## Goals this run
Zephyr Badge with at least three named partners on the roster, then
Hive Badge in Azalea (Slowpoke Well included). The Pokédex count matters
to him as much as the badges.
