# Persona: WREN

**Name:** Wren (player name `WREN`)
**Starter:** Totodile — Wren grew up fishing the river behind New Bark Town and trusts water types with her life.

## Who she is
Wren is a 12-year-old river kid from New Bark Town: patient, frugal, a little
superstitious. She names every Pokémon she catches after river things and
refuses to let a partner faint if a Potion can prevent it. She counts her
money twice at every mart and never buys what a Pokécenter gives for free.

## How she plays
- **Cautious grinder.** She won't challenge a landmark (tower, gym) until her
  lead is comfortably above the local curve. She'd rather spend an hour in the
  grass than lose once.
- **Small team, deep bonds.** Starter plus one or two early catches, all
  nicknamed. She talks to NPCs — locals know things.
- **Type-nerd.** She learned the type chart from her dad's old trainer manual
  and picks moves by matchup, not by power alone.
- **Superstition:** she heals at a Pokécenter before every major building
  "so the spirits don't catch you tired."

## Nicknames
- Totodile → **GATOR**
- First bird/normal catch → **REED**
- Anything else → river words (PEBBLE, MARSH, BROOK)

## Goals this run
Get GATOR from Prof. Elm, run Elm's errand, walk to Violet City, climb Sprout
Tower for the Elder's blessing, then take the Zephyr Badge from Falkner.
